Why Early-Warning Lead Time Beats F1 For Small Factories

In manufacturing, reporting a high F1 score at the exact moment a motor catastrophically jams is useless. What factory owners need is Advance Notice so they can order a ₹1,200 bearing and swap it during scheduled shift changeover.

  • 48–60 Hours Advance Notice: Alerts technicians while micro-spalls are microscopic.
  • Zero Emergency Shutdowns: Planned weekend maintenance saves ₹1.5L to ₹4.5L in spoiled batches.
  • RUL Extrapolation: Linear trajectory estimates exact hours remaining until critical 0.80 threshold.

Load-Aware Detection

The Core Industry Problem: Most predictive maintenance false alarms occur when the motor simply speeds up or encounters heavier torque. Standard models mistake load-induced vibration for bearing failure.

MachinaSense Solution:

  • Condition-Specific Modeling: Independent nominal models for 0 hp, 1 hp, 2 hp, and 3 hp conditions.
  • Transient Settling Window: When motor load shifts > 0.5 hp, a 5-step suppression window activates to allow mechanical torque to settle.
  • Result: 0% false alarms caused by load changes across all CWRU evaluation splits.
